### Added

- Voice mode tells a pause mid-thought from the end of what you were
  saying. When a pause doesn't sound finished, it keeps listening a little
  longer instead of answering half a question; when it does, it answers as
  soon as before. It uses Smart Turn v3, an 8.7 MB model that downloads in
  the background the first time and runs on the processor. The front door
  does the same when a visitor pauses. (#1403)
